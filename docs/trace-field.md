# A small defining polynomial for the degree-31 calculation

Let $E$ be the field generated by a root of the [Thue–Mahler form](thue-mahler.md) $F(T,1)$. For computing prime ideals it has a simpler presentation. Define $D_0=2$, $D_1=T$ and $D_n=T D_{n-1}-2D_{n-2}$. Then

$$ E\simeq\mathbb Q[T]/(D_{31}(T)-90621). $$

To see this, choose a complex 31st root $r$ of $\eta=(90621+391\sqrt{-2471})/2$. Its complex conjugate is $2/r$ since $\eta\bar\eta=2^{31}$. The field $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-2471})$ is contained in $\mathbb Q(r)$ since $\eta=r^{31}$. Over $K$ the polynomial $Z^{31}-\eta$ is irreducible. A 31st root in $K$ would generate the nonprincipal ideal $P$, because $(\eta)=P^{31}$. Taking norms of a root of any proper factor would force $\eta$ to be a 31st power, by the prime-degree argument in the descent. Hence $[\mathbb Q(r):\mathbb Q]=62$.

Put $\theta=r+2/r$. The recurrence gives $D_n(\theta)=r^n+(2/r)^n$. Thus $D_{31}(\theta)=90621$. The quadratic equation $r^2-\theta r+2=0$ now gives $[\mathbb Q(\theta):\mathbb Q]=31$. This is the fixed field of complex conjugation inside $\mathbb Q(r)$.

To identify it with the desired field, let $L_1=7+3w$, $L_2=w-83$ in the notation of the form. Put $h=(r^2/2)^8$. Then $h^{31}=(\eta/\bar\eta)^8$ and $\bar h=h^{-1}$. The element

$$ t=(h\bar L_2-L_2)/(L_1-h\bar L_1) $$

is fixed by conjugation. The denominator is nonzero, since $h\notin K$ by the ideal-class argument for $F$. By construction $(L_1t+L_2)/(\bar L_1t+\bar L_2)=h$, giving $F(t,1)=0$. Irreducibility of $F$ now identifies $\mathbb Q(t)=\mathbb Q(\theta)$. This totally real field differs from the pure radical field $\mathbb Q(2471^{1/31})$, which has only one real embedding.
